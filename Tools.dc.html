Knowledge Calibration Test

Most people can't tell the difference between "I recognise this" and "I can actually produce this from memory" - and that gap is what tanks performance under pressure.

Before reading anything - guess: does testing yourself on new material before you've studied it help you learn it better than studying it first?

Read this

Guessing wrong before you're told the answer forces a memory search that primes you to notice and retain the correct answer when it arrives - a "pretesting effect." It works even when your guess is wrong, as long as you actually attempt one.

Now - how confident are you that you could explain the pretesting effect to someone else, from memory, right now?
